Budgeting and saving are important tools for managing money and achieving financial goals. 

Here are some budgeting and saving techniques for beginners:

* Create a budget: The first step in budgeting is to create a budget that includes all of your income and expenses. This will help you understand where your money is going and identify areas where you can cut back.
* Track your spending: Tracking your spending can help you identify areas where you may be overspending and make adjustments to your budget accordingly.
* Set financial goals: Setting financial goals, such as saving for an emergency fund, paying off debt, or saving for a down payment on a house, can help you focus your budgeting and saving efforts.
* Prioritize savings: Make sure to prioritize savings in your budget, setting aside a specific amount each month for your financial goals.
* Cut unnecessary expenses: Look for ways to cut back on unnecessary expenses, such as dining out, subscriptions, or entertainment.
* Automate your savings: Automating your savings by setting up automatic transfers to a savings account can help you save money without thinking about it.
* Live below your means: Try to live below your means, by spending less than you earn, this can help you save more money.
* Take advantage of discounts and deals: Look for ways to save money on essential expenses, such as using coupons or shopping sales.
* Avoid impulse purchases: Be mindful of impulse purchases and try to stick to your budget as much as possible
* Be consistent: Consistency is key when it comes to budgeting and saving. Try to stick to your plan as much as you can.
