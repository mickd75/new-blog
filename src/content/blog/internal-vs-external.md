Nowadays, it’s about getting from point A to point B as quickly as possible, but If you achieve a physical result while neglecting everything above, the results won’t stick.

Everyone talks about and usually focuses on the physical results, but what about your internal results?

What about your [mental health](https://www.cdc.gov/mentalhealth/learn/index.htm#:~:text=Mental%20health%20includes%20our%20emotional,childhood%20and%20adolescence%20through%20adulthood.), stress levels, [gut health](https://www.healthline.com/nutrition/improve-gut-bacteria), and overall internal health?

Not only will this take more time, but ultimately, you would’ve taken a step back by not prioritizing what ACTUALLY matters.

People are trashing their metabolisms, hormone balance, and overall well-being in pursuit of this superficial goal.

Do we all want to look good shirtless? Of course, but that’s a by-product of going about the process in a sustainable way.

It’s not the be-all and end-all of your existence.

You can look good but still feel like crap.

If that’s the case, was it really worth it?

Hope this post introduces a new perspective on your progress and how you’re going about it.
