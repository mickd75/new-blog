Life doesn't always give us what we think we want...

Yet the Universe always gives us what we need.

It's funny how that happens.

Sometimes, we're so sure about how things are supposed to play out that we fight, kicking and screaming, any deviation of the plan that comes our way.

Let go of that attachment. Notice the resistance.

The more we resist, the more it persists, and the harder we make it on ourselves.

The choices we make and even the thoughts we think all matter greatly and affect the outcome of our existence

There is also another force at work and our role is actually more of a co-creator in our own lives.

We all have unique gifts to offer the world and certain passions that lie inside of us wanting to get out.

To discover our gifts and rise to our highest selves, it’s important to believe that the Universe has a plan for you and you only need to listen to the divine guidance and take inspired action.

We’re all capable of tapping into the unified field where all knowledge resides, but first, we must truly tap into ourselves.
