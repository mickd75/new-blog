The cost of travel insurance can vary depending on a number of factors, such as the length of the trip, the destination, and the type of coverage. However, on average, t**ravel insurance in the United States can cost between 4% to 10% of the total trip cost**. For example, a trip that costs $5,000 would cost between $200 and $500 for travel insurance.

The cost can also vary depending on the coverage level you choose. For example, a basic travel insurance policy that covers trip cancellation and interruption may be less expensive than a comprehensive policy that also includes medical coverage and emergency evacuation.

It is also important to **note that some credit card companies provide travel insurance as a benefit when you use their card to pay for a trip**, so it's worth checking if you have such a benefit before purchasing a separate travel insurance policy.

It's always good to shop around and compare policies and prices from different providers before purchasing travel insurance.
