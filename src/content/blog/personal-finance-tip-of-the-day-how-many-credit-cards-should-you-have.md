Deciding how many credit cards to have and which one to choose can be a daunting task. However, with careful consideration and responsible use, credit cards can be a valuable tool for building credit and earning rewards. When it comes to how many credit cards to have, it's generally recommended to start small and only have one or two credit cards to start. This allows you to focus on building a positive credit history with one or two lenders, while also keeping your credit utilization low. To choose a credit card with the best benefits, start by considering your spending habits and what rewards would be most valuable to you. For example, if you frequently travel, a travel rewards card may be a good fit for you. If you prefer cash back rewards, a cash back card may be a better fit. It's also important to consider the fees associated with the credit card, including annual fees, foreign transaction fees, and balance transfer fees. Look for a credit card with no annual fee and low fees in other areas. Finally, always pay your balance in full every month to avoid paying high interest rates and carrying a balance. This will help you avoid falling into debt and improve your credit score over time. In summary, when it comes to credit cards, start small, choose a card with benefits that align with your spending habits, and always pay your balance in full every month.
